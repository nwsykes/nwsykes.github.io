03 / Working method

Move from ambiguity to something people can use.

  1. 01Get close

    Watch the work and listen for the real problem.

  2. 02Make it visible

    Prototype the idea so teams can react to something concrete.

  3. 03Make it useful

    Refine the product around behavior, evidence, and clarity.
