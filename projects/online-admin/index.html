Online Admin

Enterprise / Platform2020

Powerful administrative features for large clients—simplified and improved applications for everyone.

01

Power without obstacles

One area I continually emphasize as a product leader is usability. An app should be simple to understand and navigate without sacrificing power. Unfortunately, some of my products had acquired obstacles in the form of administrative features built in the past.

Those features were critical to our largest customers, but spoiled usability for many others. Under my leadership we systematically enhanced and moved them into a new Online Admin.

Customers with the right security access gained deeper insight, stronger controls, configuration tools, and new rules engines. Customers in our field applications benefited from greater simplicity and more intelligent tools.

02

A configurable platform

Instance Configuration lets a company manage users, licenses, security groups, preferences, and updates. It reduced project and account-management costs while helping customers discover and add useful modules.

Profile Configuration lets large organizations modify features for a company profile, build custom forms, and enable feature sets with documentation and previews.

The Rules Editor allows customers to build complex rules that previously required weeks of development, opening new markets, partnerships, and revenue opportunities.
