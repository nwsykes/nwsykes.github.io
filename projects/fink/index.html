The Unconventional: Rat Fink

Design / Personal2006

A personal creative practice rooted in family, illustration, and unconventional art.

01

Creative roots

My dad grew up in the sixties and came to admire Rat Fink and the other creations of Ed “Big Daddy” Roth. I grew up in the eighties, learning to draw and paint at my father’s side. I grew to love classical art and painting, as well as the unconventional art found in cartoons and comic books.

One year my dad and I took a road trip to meet Ed and see his work firsthand. In hindsight it was a wonderfully ridiculous reason to travel together—and a memorable trip.

Over the years I’ve continued to draw, paint, and carefully assemble models. They sit in my office as reminders of my roots, my appreciation for my dad, and the unconventional projects I still enjoy making.

Nick Sykes / Utah / 2026
