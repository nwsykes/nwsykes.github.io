Voice Estimation

Voice / Prototype2015

Simplifying estimating with voice-to-text, creative design, and thoughtful engineering.

01

A faster way to capture work

One of the harder parts of the job is walking a site and cataloging every item. A customer must stop, search, and add each item to a project. We used the voice-to-text strengths of a mobile device to address that pain point.

We didn’t nail the design immediately. It took multiple design iterations and creative engineering, but we eventually found the sweet spot and addressed exactly what our customers needed.

02

Tap, speak, continue

A customer can find an item without navigating multiple controls or memorizing a script: tap and hold the microphone, describe what needs to be added, and move on.

Although we have since developed entirely new ways to improve this experience, voice search remains a trusted method used by many customers today.

Nick Sykes / Utah / 2026
