Insights

Product strategy / AI2024

Modernizing our property suite with Insights to expand enterprise adoption and growth.

01

Transforming the property suite

In 2023, we set out to redefine how our property suite was perceived in the industry. While we already held a significant share of the lower-end market, our goal was to position ourselves as the premier choice for enterprise customers.

We modernized the interface, expanded mobile capabilities, optimized pricing, integrated more AI-driven features, and introduced Insights in 2024. These strategic investments paid off—2024 marked our largest revenue year to date, with a record number of enterprise accounts signed and onboarded.

02

About Insights

Insights is an advanced reporting and analytics tool embedded within our property suite and PPW. It uses AI and machine learning to help businesses monitor key operational and financial metrics without requiring specialized reporting expertise.

The self-service platform lets users create, customize, and share reports directly within PPW. Users generate reports by typing a query in plain language, eliminating complex SQL queries and external analytics tools.

Natural-language search learns industry terminology, embedded analytics provides real-time analysis, and smart autocomplete personalizes recommendations based on individual and company-wide trends.

03

Visualization and impact

Users can create tailored visualizations, pin important data points to dashboards, and customize reports for sharing across teams. Liveboards provide real-time tracking of critical metrics and support informed, data-driven decision-making.

Insights empowers businesses to make data-driven decisions.
