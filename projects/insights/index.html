Product transformation

Transforming our property suite for enterprise success

In 2023, we set out to redefine how our property suite of products was perceived in the industry. While we already held a significant share of the lower-end market, our goal was to position ourselves as the premier choice for enterprise customers. To achieve this, we launched several key initiatives:

  • Modernized UI: We enhanced the user interface to provide a more polished, intuitive experience—ensuring enterprise customers saw our solution as both modern and scalable.
  • Mobile expansion: We initiated the development of a robust mobile solution to extend our platform’s capabilities.
  • Optimized pricing strategy: We refined our pricing model to better align with enterprise needs.
  • AI-powered enhancements: We integrated more AI-driven features to improve automation and decision-making.
  • Insights: In 2024, we introduced Insights, a seamlessly integrated tool with a game-changing analytics capability that became a major catalyst for growth.

These strategic investments paid off—2024 marked our largest revenue year to date, with a record number of enterprise accounts signed and onboarded.

About Insights

Business intelligence

Insights is an advanced reporting and analytics tool embedded within our property suite and the product PPW. Insights utilizes AI and machine learning to help businesses monitor key operational and financial metrics without requiring specialized reporting expertise. With Insights, users can track profitability, compliance, and analyze trends such as month-over-month or year-over-year volume, client and vendor margins, and vendor performance.

How Insights works

Insights is designed as a self-service platform, allowing users to create, customize, and share reports directly within PPW without the need for external tools or developer support.

Users can generate reports by simply typing a query in plain language. This intuitive approach eliminates the need for complex SQL queries or external analytics platforms, streamlining access to critical business insights.

Continuous learning and AI integration

Insights leverages AI to enhance usability and efficiency:

  • Natural language search: Users can input everyday language or industry-specific terms to locate data points. The system continuously learns and adapts to new terminology.
  • Embedded analytics: Insights provides real-time analysis directly within PPW rather than requiring data to be exported to another platform.
  • Smart autocomplete: Search recommendations are personalized based on the user’s history and company-wide trends, improving accuracy over time.

Designed for decisions

Custom dashboards and data visualization

Users can create tailored visualizations to monitor key business metrics, pin important data points to dashboards for quick access, and customize reports for seamless sharing across teams. With Liveboards, real-time tracking of critical metrics ensures informed and data-driven decision-making.

Insights empowers businesses to make data-driven decisions.
