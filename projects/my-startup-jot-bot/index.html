My First Tech Startup: Jot Bot

Startup / Mobile2022

A fun startup company with a strong purpose: making personal journaling easier.

01

The idea

I’ve always been a big believer in recording your history. Every year I compile a picture book of family events and put hours into editing a family home video. These artifacts of the past are incredibly special to me and my family, but journal writing can be difficult because of the time commitment required.

Several close friends and I noticed that while many of us struggled to find time for a journal, we still documented our lives online and through social media. We created a startup around that simple problem and solution—we called it Jot Bot.

02

How Jot Bot works

Jot Bot is a native iOS app with a powerful backend connecting to multiple social media APIs. A customer connects Facebook, Instagram, or Twitter and imports previous content as an immediate private journal. They can then continue writing or import future social posts.

We created a visual tagging and search feature called jot dots, a calendar for seeing entries and filling gaps, and prompts that turn photos into new journal entries.

Jot Bot lets someone quickly capture a moment on a phone or move to an iPad for a detailed keyboard entry. Everything syncs and saves with AWS so the journal remains backed up and secure.
