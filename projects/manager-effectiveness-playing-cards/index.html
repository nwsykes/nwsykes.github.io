Leadership made tangible

This card deck grew from a year-long executive leadership training program. It turns ideas about manager effectiveness into a visual, approachable tool that can be held, shared, discussed, and revisited.

The result brings creative practice into leadership development—using illustration and design to make important principles memorable.
