The story behind
the work.

Our company produced a series of promotional videos showcasing how XactRemodel helps solve common challenges faced by contractors and remodelers. The series also included quick training materials that could be cut up and referenced later. The campaign was an incredible success, resulting in many new trial sign-ups.

The first video was a short pre-roll focused primarily on promotion. The second featured two concerned homeowners (played by my wife and me) navigating a kitchen remodel—even starring our dog, Hendrix.

Remember: “Happiness Drives Profits.”
