Frontline Obsession

Customer research / Film2023

Project context

The story behind
the work.

In 2023 I traveled with two colleagues to Carlsbad, CA. We were on a mission to the front lines, shadowing our customers as they used ContentsTrack to pack out, clean, and store home contents during the restoration process. We wanted to see my product, ContentsTrack—a mobile app and back-office web application—being used in real-world scenarios. Our goal was to gather feedback, understand customer needs, and find ways to improve the product.

The video, titled Frontline Obsession, captures my passion for building amazing products through customer collaboration.

Nick Sykes / Utah / 2026
